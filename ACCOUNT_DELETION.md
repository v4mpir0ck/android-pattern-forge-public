# Account Deletion Request

**Last Updated:** October 23, 2025

We respect your right to privacy and data control. This document provides clear instructions on how to request the permanent deletion of your Pattern Forge account and all associated data using our official request form.

---

## How to Request Account Deletion

To permanently delete your account and all personal data associated with it, you must submit a request through our secure Google Form.

**Please follow these steps:**

1.  **Click the link below** to open the official Account Deletion Request Form.
    *   **[Go to Account Deletion Form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfMU7ZVYa8MD-Vdi6_0d29Yz53V8sv8kob2tp5iQ2rSH1NDnA/viewform?usp=sharing&ouid=108331823525638582364)**

2.  **Fill out the form.** You will be asked to provide and confirm the email address associated with your Pattern Forge account. It is crucial that you use the correct email address for us to be able to identify your account.

---

## What Happens Next?

- **Verification:** Our support team will receive your form submission. We will verify that the request is coming from an email address associated with an existing account.
- **Processing:** Once verified, we will initiate the deletion process. This process is irreversible and will permanently remove all your personal information, including your email, username, generated patterns, and purchase history from our systems.
- **Confirmation:** The deletion process may take up to 30 days to complete. We will send a final confirmation email to you once your data has been permanently erased.

**Important Note:** Deleting the app from your device does not delete your account. You must submit the form to ensure your data is removed from our servers.

---

If you have any questions about this process, please contact us at `support@pattern-forge.com`.
